Where's your media?

Add the folders holding your footage and your separately-recorded audio. They can live on different drives.

External drive? Use Add folder — it opens your system chooser, which also unlocks protected drives.

Set up your tracks

Mark your separate audio recordings as reference — everything else gets synced to them. For each camera, open Channels to see and hear what its audio actually contains.

Check the sync

Green is locked to the sample. Amber is within about half a second. Red couldn't be matched — worth a look. Click any clip to hear and see it against its reference.

Send it to your editor

Exports a timeline XML that imports into Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve, Final Cut 7 and Media Composer — plus a placement CSV and an optional Resolve script. Nothing touches your media.

Premiere Pro

File › Import… → pick synced_timeline.xml. Premiere builds a sequence with your tracks and clip positions intact.

DaVinci Resolve

File › Import › Timeline › Import AAF/EDL/XML… → the same XML. (Or run the exported Python script in Workspace › Console — that route also applies your metadata and clip colours.)
